This Privacy Policy describes what data Next Level Security Systems collects and how it is used for employee customer-care SMS.

Information we collect

When you opt in to SMS text messages from Next Level Security Systems, we collect:

  • Your mobile phone number
  • The text messages you send to our company number
  • Message timestamps, delivery status, and opt-in / opt-out records

We use this information only to reply to employee IT and operations questions, deliver SMS, and keep consent records required by carriers. Frequency is 1 message per employee request.

SMS opt-in data

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

We may share data with carriers and Twilio solely to deliver SMS. We do not sell lists. We do not use SMS opt-in data for advertising. This is a first-party program for Next Level Security Systems employees. It is not lead generation.

Retention

We keep opt-in and opt-out records as required for A2P 10DLC and TCPA compliance. Message content is stored only as needed to operate employee customer-care SMS.
